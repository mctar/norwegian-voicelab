RESEARCH NOTES · 27 OCTOBER 2026

What we’re trying to learn.

Read the full brief ↗
01

Norwegian ASR has moved on.

NB-Whisper is the established reference. NbAiLab’s new Qwen-based Bokfink family adds explicit written-standard and verbatim controls, vocabulary hints and long/sparse-speech training. Version 0.2.1 was created on 25 October.

Its published long-form cohort favours 1.7B over 0.6B (18.27% versus 23.90% WER), but contains only seven recordings. That is a candidate-selection signal, not a customer-service result.

Read the model card ↗
02

Dialect ≠ written standard.

A Bergen or Tromsø speaker can request a Bokmål or Nynorsk transcript. Score the requested writing standard independently of the spoken dialect.

Read speech, parliamentary speech and a clear microphone do not establish performance on phone calls. We need native speakers across five broad dialect regions, noisy audio, hesitations, names, numbers and silence.

NB Samtale corpus ↗
03

Pick a stack for the demo.

For a transparent local experiment: NB-Whisper + Borealis2 + Piper, with Bokfink 0.2 alongside. Enable 0.2.1 after access approval and compare it on the same audio.

For a polished hosted voice-agent demo, ElevenLabs is a sensible comparator; Speechmatics is another STT candidate. Norwegian support is documented. A dialect-quality winner still needs our recordings.

On one 3-second silence probe, upstream Whisper Turbo generated text; the NbAiLab profiles returned empty output. These are functional observations, not quality-rate estimates.

The test that matters

East · West · Trøndelag · North · South. Start with at least three speakers per region and the same ten customer-service tasks. Record normal speech first; test names, dates, amounts, corrections, silence, background noise and a request for a person.

Measure task completion and critical-field errors alongside word errors, time from the end of speech to the first audible reply, and interruption/handoff behaviour. Do not infer customer preference for bots from a speech benchmark.

Published scores and our own measurements are kept separate. This lab does not claim to have measured dialect coverage yet.
